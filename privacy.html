托育術術過（CarePass）隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 26 日

「托育術術過」（以下簡稱本 App）是托育人員技術士術科的練習工具。本 App 不需要註冊帳號，所有功能都在您的裝置上離線執行，不會把任何資料傳送到開發者或第三方的伺服器。

1. 麥克風

  • 術科練習時，本 App 使用麥克風辨識您口述的關鍵字，並偵測水龍頭的開關聲，以自動提示下一個步驟。
  • 語音在裝置上即時辨識，辨識完即捨棄，不會錄音存檔，也不會上傳。
  • 只有在您按下「開始收音」後才會使用麥克風；按下「停止收音」或離開練習即停止。
  • 「系統狀態」頁的「環境音監測」是測試用功能：只有在您勾選「同時存成 WAV 檔」並開始監測時，才會把錄音存在本 App 專屬的儲存空間，不會上傳。

2. 相機與照片

  • 「切丁大小」功能讓您拍照或選擇一張照片，在裝置上量測每顆丁的大小，不會上傳照片。
  • 拍照時使用裝置內建的相機 App；選照片時只能讀取您選擇的那一張。
  • 測試期間，「同時存下原始照片與量測結果」選項預設為開啟：照片與量測結果會存在本 App 專屬的儲存空間，供量測準確度驗證使用，不會上傳。您可以在拍照前取消勾選。

3. 存在裝置上的資料

  • 本 App 使用裝置上的資料庫與 App 專屬的儲存空間保存資料，這些資料只存在您的裝置上。
  • 「系統狀態」頁會顯示裝置型號與作業系統版本，僅供畫面顯示，不會傳送出去。
  • 解除安裝本 App 時，以上資料會一併刪除。

4. 不收集、不分享

  • 本 App 不收集姓名、電子郵件、電話、位置、聯絡人等個人資料。
  • 本 App 不含廣告，也沒有使用任何數據分析或追蹤服務。
  • 本 App 不會與任何第三方分享資料。

5. 適用對象

本 App 的使用對象是準備托育人員技術士術科測試的成年考生，不以兒童為對象。

6. 政策變更

本政策如有變更，將更新於本頁面並修改生效日期。

7. 聯絡方式

對本政策有任何疑問，請來信：lai.chiaping@gmail.com

本 App 為個人開發的練習工具，非勞動部或任何官方機構的 App。

CarePass (托育術術過) Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

CarePass ("the App") is a practice tool for the practical exam of the Taiwan Childcare Worker skills certification. The App requires no account. All features run offline on your device, and no data is sent to the developer's or any third party's servers.

1. Microphone

  • During practice, the App uses the microphone to recognize the keywords you speak and to detect the sound of a running faucet, so it can prompt the next step automatically.
  • Speech is recognized on the device in real time and discarded afterwards. It is not recorded or uploaded.
  • The microphone is used only after you tap "Start listening" (開始收音), and stops when you tap "Stop listening" (停止收音) or leave the practice.
  • The "Ambient sound monitor" (環境音監測) on the "System status" (系統狀態) page is a testing feature. Only when you check "Also save as WAV" (同時存成 WAV 檔) and start monitoring are recordings saved to the App's private storage. They are not uploaded.

2. Camera and photos

  • The "Dice size" (切丁大小) feature lets you take or choose a photo and measures each diced piece on the device. Photos are not uploaded.
  • Taking a photo uses your device's built-in camera app. When choosing a photo, the App can read only the photo you select.
  • During testing, the option "Also save the original photo and results" (同時存下原始照片與量測結果) is on by default: the photo and measurement results are saved to the App's private storage to verify measurement accuracy, and are not uploaded. You can uncheck it before taking a photo.

3. Data stored on your device

  • The App stores data in an on-device database and in the App's private storage. This data stays on your device only.
  • The "System status" page shows your device model and OS version for display only. This information is not sent anywhere.
  • All of the above data is deleted when you uninstall the App.

4. No collection, no sharing

  • The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email, phone number, location, or contacts.
  • The App contains no ads and uses no analytics or tracking services.
  • The App does not share any data with third parties.

5. Intended audience

The App is intended for adult candidates preparing for the Childcare Worker practical exam. It is not directed at children.

6. Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please email lai.chiaping@gmail.com.

The App is a personally developed practice tool and is not an official app of the Ministry of Labor or any government agency.
