托育術術過（CarePass）隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日

「托育術術過」（以下簡稱本 App）是托育人員技術士術科的練習工具。本 App 不需要註冊帳號，所有功能都在您的裝置上離線執行。唯一會傳送出去的是錯誤報告（見第 4 節），您可以隨時關閉。

1. 麥克風

  • 術科練習時，本 App 使用麥克風辨識您口述的關鍵字，並偵測水龍頭的開關聲，以自動提示下一個步驟。
  • 語音在裝置上即時辨識，辨識完即捨棄，不會錄音存檔，也不會上傳。
  • 只有在您按下「開始收音」後才會使用麥克風；按下「停止收音」或離開練習即停止。
  • 「系統狀態」頁的「環境音監測」是測試用功能：只有在您勾選「同時存成 WAV 檔」並開始監測時，才會把錄音存在本 App 專屬的儲存空間，不會上傳。

2. 相機與照片

  • 「切丁大小」功能讓您拍照或選擇一張照片，在裝置上量測每顆丁的大小，不會上傳照片。
  • 拍照時使用裝置內建的相機 App；選照片時只能讀取您選擇的那一張。
  • 測試期間，「同時存下原始照片與量測結果」選項預設為開啟：照片與量測結果會存在本 App 專屬的儲存空間，供量測準確度驗證使用，不會上傳。您可以在拍照前取消勾選。

3. 存在裝置上的資料

  • 本 App 使用裝置上的資料庫與 App 專屬的儲存空間保存資料，這些資料只存在您的裝置上。
  • 「系統狀態」頁會顯示裝置型號與作業系統版本，僅供畫面顯示；錯誤報告開啟時，報告中會附上這兩項資訊（見第 4 節）。
  • 解除安裝本 App 時，以上資料會一併刪除。

4. 錯誤報告（Android）

  • 測試期間，「傳送錯誤報告」預設為開啟：本 App 發生錯誤或閃退時，會把錯誤報告傳送到 GlitchTip（錯誤追蹤服務），供開發者修正問題。
  • 錯誤報告包含：錯誤訊息與發生位置、App 版本、裝置型號、作業系統版本，以及出錯前的操作紀錄（例如切換了哪個頁面、按了哪個按鈕）。
  • 錯誤報告不包含姓名、帳號等可識別身分的資訊，也不包含錄音、語音辨識的文字或照片。
  • 您可以在「系統狀態」頁的「錯誤報告」關閉「傳送錯誤報告」；關閉後立即停止傳送（閃退的報告在下次開啟本 App 後停止）。
  • 正式版會改成預設關閉。

5. 不收集、不分享

  • 本 App 不收集姓名、電子郵件、電話、位置、聯絡人等個人資料。
  • 本 App 目前沒有廣告，也沒有使用數據分析或行為追蹤服務；第 4 節的錯誤報告只用於修正問題。
  • 之後的版本預計加入廣告（橫幅廣告，以及查看練習總報告前播放的短影片廣告），並可能記錄 App 的使用情形以改善功能。加入之前會先更新本政策，說明使用的服務、收集的資料，以及您可以做的選擇。
  • 除了第 4 節的錯誤報告由 GlitchTip 代為保存之外，本 App 不會與任何第三方分享資料。

6. 適用對象

本 App 的使用對象是準備托育人員技術士術科測試的成年考生，不以兒童為對象。

7. 政策變更

本政策如有變更，將更新於本頁面並修改生效日期。

8. 聯絡方式

對本政策有任何疑問，請來信：lai.chiaping@gmail.com

本 App 為個人開發的練習工具，非勞動部或任何官方機構的 App。

CarePass (托育術術過) Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

CarePass ("the App") is a practice tool for the practical exam of the Taiwan Childcare Worker skills certification. The App requires no account, and all features run offline on your device. The only data sent off the device is error reports (see Section 4), which you can turn off at any time.

1. Microphone

  • During practice, the App uses the microphone to recognize the keywords you speak and to detect the sound of a running faucet, so it can prompt the next step automatically.
  • Speech is recognized on the device in real time and discarded afterwards. It is not recorded or uploaded.
  • The microphone is used only after you tap "Start listening" (開始收音), and stops when you tap "Stop listening" (停止收音) or leave the practice.
  • The "Ambient sound monitor" (環境音監測) on the "System status" (系統狀態) page is a testing feature. Only when you check "Also save as WAV" (同時存成 WAV 檔) and start monitoring are recordings saved to the App's private storage. They are not uploaded.

2. Camera and photos

  • The "Dice size" (切丁大小) feature lets you take or choose a photo and measures each diced piece on the device. Photos are not uploaded.
  • Taking a photo uses your device's built-in camera app. When choosing a photo, the App can read only the photo you select.
  • During testing, the option "Also save the original photo and results" (同時存下原始照片與量測結果) is on by default: the photo and measurement results are saved to the App's private storage to verify measurement accuracy, and are not uploaded. You can uncheck it before taking a photo.

3. Data stored on your device

  • The App stores data in an on-device database and in the App's private storage. This data stays on your device only.
  • The "System status" page shows your device model and OS version for display. When error reporting is on, error reports include these two items (see Section 4).
  • All of the above data is deleted when you uninstall the App.

4. Error reports (Android)

  • During testing, "Send error reports" (傳送錯誤報告) is on by default: when the App hits an error or crashes, it sends an error report to GlitchTip, an error tracking service, so the developer can fix the problem.
  • An error report contains the error message and where it occurred, the App version, your device model, OS version, and the actions taken before the error (for example, which page was opened or which button was tapped).
  • Error reports do not contain your name, account, or other identifying information, and do not contain recordings, recognized speech text, or photos.
  • You can turn off "Send error reports" under "Error reports" (錯誤報告) on the "System status" page. Sending stops immediately (crash reports stop after the App is next opened).
  • The release version will have error reporting off by default.

5. No collection, no sharing

  • The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email, phone number, location, or contacts.
  • The App currently contains no ads and uses no analytics or behavior tracking services. The error reports in Section 4 are used only to fix problems.
  • A future version is planned to include ads (banner ads, and a short video ad before the practice summary report is shown) and may record how the App is used in order to improve it. Before that happens, this policy will be updated to describe the services used, the data collected, and the choices available to you.
  • Apart from the error reports in Section 4, which are stored by GlitchTip, the App does not share any data with third parties.

6. Intended audience

The App is intended for adult candidates preparing for the Childcare Worker practical exam. It is not directed at children.

7. Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
